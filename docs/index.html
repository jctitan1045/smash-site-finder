What the types mean

Local: a shop-style commercial space, usually ground floor with a street front (retail, restaurant, gym, showroom). Closest to a "storefront".

Bodega: literally "warehouse", but in Poblado/Envigado it usually means a large open commercial box: high ceilings, few walls, truck door, often industrial power. Rawer and cheaper per m², more build-out needed, but the easiest shell for a gym + sauna floor.

Edificio: a whole building, all floors (like Cra 47B). Often a former office block, hotel or mixed-use. Most are for sale; the ones for rent give you the whole stack.
